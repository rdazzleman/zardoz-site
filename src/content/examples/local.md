**Security Operations** must provide incident response training to users based on roles and responsibilities. Training must include initial training within organization-defined timeframes (e.g., one month for ARC-AMPE), subsequent training at organization-defined frequencies (e.g., annually for ARC-AMPE), and review/update of training content at organization-defined frequencies or following events. Training must include simulated events, automated training environments, and breach-specific training.
