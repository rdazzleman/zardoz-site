Provide incident response training to system users consistent with roles and responsibilities, including within organization-defined time periods of assuming roles, when required by system changes, and at organization-defined frequencies thereafter. Review and update training content at organization-defined frequencies and following events. ARC-AMPE requires training within one month of assuming roles and every year thereafter.
