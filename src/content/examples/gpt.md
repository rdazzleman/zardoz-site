- Spiders from Mars must provide system users incident response training consistent with their assigned roles and responsibilities, including when system changes require training. Initial training must occur within one month of a user assuming an incident response role or responsibility or acquiring system access, and subsequent training must occur within one year thereafter. Spiders from Mars must formally maintain and record which personnel participate. [NIST IR-2 | FedRAMP | ARC-AMPE]
