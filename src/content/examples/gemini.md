Spiders from Mars must provide incident response training to system users consistent with assigned roles and responsibilities when required by system changes.
[NIST IR-2 | FedRAMP | ARC-AMPE]

Spiders from Mars must provide incident response training within one (1) month of assuming an incident response role/responsibility or acquiring system access, and within one (1) year thereafter.
[NIST IR-2 | FedRAMP] [ARC-AMPE IR-2 AE Mandatory]
