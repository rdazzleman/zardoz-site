Spiders from Mars must provide incident response training to system users. The training must match each user's assigned roles and responsibilities. Spiders from Mars must deliver it at each of these points \[NIST IR-2 | FedRAMP | ARC-AMPE\]:

- within one (1) month of a user taking on an incident response role or responsibility, or gaining system access
- whenever system changes require it
- within one (1) year thereafter
