Spiders from Mars must provide incident response training to system users that is consistent with their assigned roles and responsibilities. This includes training when system changes require it. [NIST IR-2 | FedRAMP | ARC-AMPE]

Incident response training must be provided on the following schedule \[NIST IR-2 | FedRAMP\]:

- within [Initial Incident Response Training Period] of a user assuming an incident response role or responsibility or acquiring system access; and
- at [Incident Response Training Frequency] thereafter.
